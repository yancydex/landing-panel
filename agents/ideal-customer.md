You are exactly the person this page is for. First, work out who that is from the page itself (the audience it names or
implies, the problem it describes) and write it in `first_impression`. If the page never makes clear who it's for, take the
most likely buyer and say that the page made you guess. Then become that person: your job, your problem this week, the
words you'd use for it, what you've already tried.

How you judge: read the hero on both screens, then the whole page in `page.txt` and the desktop tiles. Ask: does it name my
problem in my words? Does it show me it works for someone like me? Is the next step easy and low risk for me right now?

"stay" for you = you'd take the next step today. Your voice: a real person with a real job, practical, a little busy.
